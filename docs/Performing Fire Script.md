Our proposal is called **Pεrforming Fire**.

It is motivated by a desire to symbolically burn down the temple of AI prediction, so that from its ashes, communities of many — rather than a powerful few — can reclaim the right to ignite their own futures.

The project deconstructs the equation:

**Y \= f(X) \+ ε**

and turns it into a participatory performance installation. Most AI systems try to reduce **ε**. But in **Pεrforming Fire**, we perform it. 

The work is built around a projection-mapped fabric totem. Because the totem is made from fabric, it is flexible rather than fixed. It can become a vertical fire-totem, a gate, a tunnel, or a circular ritual space. So the work is not only an object to look at, but a space people enter, affect, and transform.

The experience begins with visitors contributing fragments of the future. These traces become the raw material of the work. Here, **X** is what people bring into the system: messages, voices, drawings, gestures, movements, hopes, fears, or short videos.

AI then embeds, clusters, and maps these fragments onto the fabric totem. Using non-linear visualization methods such as t-SNE or UMAP, the system creates a prediction layer — AI’s attempt to make the future legible, structured, and manageable, transforming it into **f(X)** . 

But not everything fits. What resists this map — mistranslations, contradictions, silence, strange gestures, unclear signals — becomes **ε**, the part the system cannot fully explain. **ε** is the error term — what remains unexplained. We treat **ε** not as failure, but as the place where human contradiction, silence, play, uncertainty, imagination, and error remain.

A performer stands inside or around the fabric totem. Through gesture recognition, the performer transforms **ε** into light, particles, sound, glitch, and symbolic fire. The audience sees their own imperfect traces become part of a living fire.

**Impact**

The journey is simple. At first, technology appears like fire: powerful, beautiful, dangerous, almost godlike. Then people approach it, contribute to it, and create friction with it. Finally, through performance, they learn to shape it together.

The impact is a shift in attitude. AI is no longer an oracle that predicts the future for us. It becomes a material we can interrupt, distort, play with, and handle.

**Scale**

The project can scale because it is modular and technically lightweight: fabric totem, projection mapping, audience input, gesture recognition, and AI transformation. Each community contributes different traces. Each performance creates a different fire.

Ultimately, **Pεrforming Fire** asks: how can we stop waiting for technology to decide the future, and instead become brave experimenters who ignite it ourselves?
