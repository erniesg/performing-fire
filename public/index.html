CH 01 ABOUT 01 / EQUATION

01 / EQUATION

Y = f(X) + ε

X is what people bring. f(X) is the system's attempt to organize it. ε is what will not stay put.

02 / INPUT

X / TRACES

People bring messages, voices, drawings, gestures, movements, hopes, fears, and short videos. These traces are X: the raw material.

03 / MAP

f(X) / THE MAP

The proposed system embeds, clusters, and maps the traces. f(X) is its attempt to make them legible and manageable.

04 / REMAINDER

ε / WHAT RESISTS

Mistranslations, contradictions, silence, strange gestures, and unclear signals resist the map. Most AI systems reduce ε. Here, we perform it.

05 / PERFORMANCE

THE REMAINDER BURNS

A performer works inside or around the fabric totem, changing ε into light, particles, sound, glitch, and symbolic fire.

01 / SOURCE REGISTRY

WHAT ENTERS THE CORPUS

The Performing Fire corpus is organized as a source registry: Nam June Paik Art Center records, the separate Video Archive, the Video Library, the official Nam June Paik Art Center YouTube channel, and the ANTIEGG Fluxus article. Each entry is named before it is selected.

02 / BOUNDS

METADATA, NOT A MEDIA DUMP

The working corpus keeps bounded public metadata and source links. Raw clips, images, and private material stay out of the repository.

03 / GOVERNANCE

RIGHTS CHANGE BY OPERATION

Viewing, quoting, deriving, and publishing are separate operations. A source can be searchable for research without being copied, transformed, or published.

04 / PROVENANCE

SEARCH WITH A TRACE

Search follows provenance—source, operation, and status—before a deliberate selection is made for a visual study. The result is a considered subset, not an automatic dump.

05 / STATE

HELD / OFFLINE / PROVEN

Uncleared material stays held. Implemented offline means local metadata and fixtures only. Live-proven means a public path has been checked; research status never implies a finished model.
