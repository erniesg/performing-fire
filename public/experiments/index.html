    01 / INSTALLATION EXPERIMENT

    FABRIC · FIRE WITHOUT HARDWARE

    Hardware gives the installation its physical presence, but fabric is difficult to control: it drifts, folds, and resists every repeatable cue. Fabric asks whether software can stage those same behaviours instead. The surface follows a hand, gathers tension, and releases it as fire-like motion.

    We are not trying to make fabric obedient. We are testing how a simulated material can stay unstable enough to perform.
