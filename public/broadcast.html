HOW WILL WE GREET NEW TECHNOLOGY?

우리는 새로운 기술을 어떻게 맞이할 것인가?

Performing Fire is an interactive media installation that gathers what artists think and feel about technology — questions, hopes, fears, imaginations — and turns them into a single living fire. Each offering becomes its own flame, with its own shape, motion, and light. Over the exhibition the fire grows: a collective totem of possible futures.

TRANSMISSION NOTES · 방송 원고

The future of technology is usually imagined in two directions: possibility and threat. Performing Fire starts somewhere else — from the unstable relationship in which humans change technology and are changed by it in return. Fire was the first technology we never fully tamed; we learned instead to live alongside its instability. Perhaps what we need now is not fear of the new, nor unconditional acceptance, but 불장난 — fire play: an experimental relationship with what can affect us but cannot be fully predicted or controlled.

SIGNAL PATH · 송출 경로

  1. 01 OFFER 공물 At the installation (or here), tell us your question, hope, or fear about technology. Write it, speak it, or show it.
  2. 02 TRANSFORM 지시문 AI reads each offering — its meaning, temperature, texture — and, in the spirit of Nam June Paik and Fluxus, turns it into a Score: an open proposition anyone can perform in their own way.
  3. 03 BURN 연소 Each offering becomes a flame, projected on translucent fabric, shaped by the words that made it. Different perspectives overlap, collide, and form one collective fire.
  4. 04 PERFORM 수행 In the final weeks, a live performer reinterprets the accumulated voices and Scores with the body — reimagining 달집태우기 (moon-house burning) and 쥐불놀이 (fire play), East Asian rites where communities gathered around fire to wish and to let go.

● TEST CARD · 지시문

AI reads each offering and, in the spirit of Nam June Paik and Fluxus, returns a Score — an open proposition anyone can perform in their own way. Eight samples on rotation.

SCORES

지시문

SCORE No.01

IGNITION 점화

Write the technology you fear on your palm. Hold it over something warm. Wait until the warmth reaches the word.

SCORE No.02

MOON HOUSE 달집

Build a small house from things you planned to throw away. Put one wish inside. Do not burn it yet.

SCORE No.03

FEED 공급

Tell your oldest fear to the youngest machine in your home. Note which of you changes.

SCORE No.04

ORBIT 쥐불

Swing a light in a circle until it becomes a line. This is how fast a fear must move to look like hope.

SCORE No.05

ERROR TERM 오차항

Do something one hundred times. Keep only the flicker.

SCORE No.06

BROADCAST 송출

Hum for thirty seconds to an appliance. If it hums back, you have made a duet. If not, you have made a prayer.

SCORE No.07

HANDOVER 인계

Let a machine finish your sentence. Finish one of its own. Call it even.

SCORE No.08

ASH 재

Delete something you made. Describe what remains.
