# Performing Fire — Product Truth

## Product

Performing Fire is an evolving performance and public research broadcast about what escapes the systems people build. Its public site is a five-channel CRT console: About, Contribute, Experiments, Research, and Log.

This document records product truth approved directly by the project owner on 2026-08-28. It is not a substitute for source rights, provenance, or evidence records.

## Editorial argument

The site is one continuous artist statement, not a collection of unrelated page fragments:

1. **About** states the artistic premise: technology is made and depended upon, but never fully controlled. Fire gathers, sustains, destroys, and transforms.
2. **Contribute** explains how human thoughts, voices, and gestures enter the work as situated material rather than anonymous data.
3. **Experiments** makes the project’s thinking visible through versioned studies and explains why each version exists.
4. **Research** shows the source universe, singles out score candidates, and keeps provenance and publication limits attached to every sample.
5. **Log** records what was tested, what changed, and what becomes the next experiment.

The central relation is `Y = f(X) + ε`: `X` is what people bring, `f(X)` is the system’s attempt to organise it, and `ε` is what refuses to fit. Most AI systems try to reduce that remainder. Performing Fire gives it a body.

## Voice

- Concrete, unsentimental, and source-grounded.
- Use specific verbs and objects instead of generic “future technology,” “signal,” or “living totem” language.
- Describe work honestly as live, local, held, proposed, in development, or verified.
- Never imply that a pipeline, score, readable derivative, or public right exists before evidence supports it.

## Experiment lineage

- **Fabric v0 — Material:** direct hand-and-mouse disturbance of a fire-like software surface.
- **Fabric v1 — Audio-reactive:** microphone and generated-beat routing map bass, body, treble, and hits into explode, dissolve, glitter, and glitch parameters.
- **Fabric 2.0 — Performance composition:** an in-development cue/score system that composes intact, dissolve, particle burst, glitter, glitch, and recovery states into timed, repeatable performance sequences. It is not a duplicate of Fabric v1 and must not be presented as implemented.
- **Microsite — Broadcast form:** the visual study that developed separate screen objects into the five-channel console.

Fabric v0 remains canonical at `/experiments/fabric/`. Fabric v1 remains at `/experiments/fabric-v1/`. The retired Flame Cloth name must not return.

## Research presentation

Research stays inside CH04 of the existing broadcast. The public surface presents a rights-aware snapshot, not a raw-media dump or a complete hosted archive.

- Scores are a first-class research strand.
- Historic score candidates are distinct from future Performing Fire-generated scores.
- “Score candidate” means a collection record has been verified but its score/instruction text has not yet been human-confirmed.
- Sample records show metadata and source links. Readable files or derivatives appear only when current policy permits public rendering.
- Counts retain their observation dates and must not be summed when categories overlap.
- Every record retains source, medium, rights/display status, and provenance.

## Non-goals for this iteration

- Republishing the complete corpus or downloaded media.
- Claiming that score text has been fully identified or verified.
- Implementing the Fabric 2.0 effects/cue engine.
- Changing the current Fabric v1 runtime or its uncommitted audio-control work.
